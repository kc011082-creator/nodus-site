Questions

Pricing questions

What counts as a user?

Anyone with their own office login — sales, admin, accounts, owners. Warehouse scanner accounts only see scan and pick screens and don't count. Your customers never count.

Do you charge per order or by volume?

No. Your price depends on your plan, not how many orders you take. Grow your order book without your bill growing with it.

Why do I need Operations for more than one site?

Moving stock between sites needs every batch tracked through the transfer, so traceability holds end to end. That's built into Operations.

What does onboarding include?

A one-off £950. We load your product catalogue, customers and price lists, set up your team, and get the warehouse scanning — with you, not from a help article.

Can I change plan later?

Yes. Move up when you add staff or a site. Just email info@nodusapp.co.uk.
